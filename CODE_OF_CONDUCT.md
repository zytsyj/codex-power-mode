# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Discuss the work rather than the person, assume good intent while addressing concrete impact, and make space for different experience levels and accessibility needs.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate privacy violations, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its community.

Until a public reporting channel is approved, conduct concerns should be raised privately with the repository owner.
